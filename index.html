NoRoadBack Publisher

Internal publishing automation for the YouTube channel @noroadback.

Uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing this application you are also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

What this tool does

NoRoadBack Publisher is a private, single-operator tool that automates the publishing workflow of the travel YouTube channel @noroadback. It takes finished video files, titles, descriptions, tags, chapters, thumbnails, captions and multilingual metadata that the channel owner has prepared locally, and submits them to YouTube on the owner’s behalf through the official YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API.

Typical weekly workload on the owner’s channel

  • 1 long-form video per week (horizontal, 16:9)
  • ~7 short teasers per week derived from the long-form video
  • ~14 Shorts per week (vertical, 9:16)
  • Captions and localized titles/descriptions in multiple languages per video

Who can use it

The tool is operated by one person — the owner of the @noroadback channel — and runs on that owner’s own machine. There are no end users other than the channel owner. No account other than the channel owner’s Google account is ever asked for authorization.

What permissions it requests

The tool uses the following OAuth 2.0 scopes against the channel owner’s Google account:

ScopeWhy it is needed
youtubeCreate, update and read playlists and video metadata on the owner’s channel.
youtube.uploadUpload finished long-form videos, teasers and Shorts to the owner’s channel.
youtube.readonlyRead channel/video state (status, publish time, existing localizations) to decide what to publish or update next.
youtube.force-sslSet thumbnails and manage captions on the owner’s videos.
yt-analytics.readonlyRead non-monetary analytics (views, watch time, retention) for the owner’s own channel to inform the publishing plan.

How your data is handled

  • The only Google account the tool ever accesses is the channel owner’s own.
  • The OAuth refresh token is stored locally in a .env file on the owner’s machine. It is never transmitted to any third party and never leaves that machine.
  • No video content, metadata or analytics data is sold, shared or sent to any server other than Google’s YouTube APIs.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke this application’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After revocation the application can no longer act on the channel.
